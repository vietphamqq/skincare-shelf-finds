# About Skincare Shelf Finds

Skincare Shelf Finds is a small independent content project that publishes clear, self-contained
skincare guides — routine order, ingredient basics, realistic timelines, and budget-friendly picks.
Every guide is written to be useful on its own, without clickbait or exaggerated promises.

## Editorial approach

- Only well-established, mainstream skincare information — no medical or treatment claims.
- No "before and after" promises and no invented statistics or prices.
- Every guide is complete on its own; we do not publish teaser posts that withhold the answer.
- Any affiliate relationship is disclosed clearly wherever it applies.
