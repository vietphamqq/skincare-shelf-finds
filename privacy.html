Privacy Policy

Skincare Shelf Finds · Last updated: 27 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Skincare Shelf Finds ("we", "us") handles personal data in connection with this website and with our private Pinterest publishing application (the "Application"). We are based in Finland (European Union) and process personal data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data controller

Skincare Shelf Finds, an independent content project operated by an individual publisher based in Finland. Contact: phamquangquocviet@gmail.com.

2. Scope: what the Application does

The Application is a private, single-operator tool. Its only purpose is to create, schedule and publish our own pins to our own Pinterest business account, and to read the performance statistics of those pins. The Application is not offered to other users, has no end users other than the operator, and does not read, collect or store the Pins, boards or profile data of any other Pinterest user.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal data.
  • We do not use advertising trackers, analytics cookies or profiling on this website.
  • We do not access other Pinterest users' private data.
  • We do not use Pinterest data to train machine-learning models.
  • We do not transfer Pinterest data to any third party, except as required by law.

5. Legal basis

Where we process personal data, we rely on our legitimate interest in operating and maintaining our own publishing account (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), on consent where you contact us voluntarily (Article 6(1)(a)), and on compliance with legal obligations where applicable (Article 6(1)(c)).

6. Third-party services

We rely on a small number of service providers whose own privacy policies apply to data they process: Pinterest (publishing platform and API), GitHub (static website hosting), and Pexels (licensed stock photography used in our images).

7. Security

API credentials and access tokens are stored privately by the operator, are never published in public code repositories, and are transmitted only over encrypted HTTPS connections. Access can be revoked at any time from the Pinterest account settings, which immediately invalidates the Application's access.

8. Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the right to request access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate data, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and to object to processing. To exercise any of these rights, email phamquangquocviet@gmail.com; we aim to respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority — in Finland, the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto).

9. Children

This website and the Application are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Affiliate disclosure

Some content published by Skincare Shelf Finds may in future contain affiliate links, meaning we may earn a commission from qualifying purchases at no additional cost to you. Any such relationship is disclosed on the content itself. Affiliate programmes may use their own cookies, governed by their own privacy policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the project develops. The "last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
