What this is

Skincare Shelf Finds is a small independent content project that publishes clear, self-contained skincare guides — routine order, ingredient basics, realistic timelines, and budget-friendly picks. Every guide is written to be useful on its own, without clickbait or exaggerated promises.

Where to find us

Our guides are published as visual pins on Pinterest: Skincare Shelf Finds on Pinterest.

About our Pinterest publishing tool

We maintain a small private application that connects to the Pinterest API to schedule and publish our own pins to our own Pinterest business account. It is used solely for our own content and does not offer services to third parties, does not read other Pinterest users' data, and does not sell or share any data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Editorial approach

  • Only well-established, mainstream skincare information — no medical or treatment claims.
  • No "before and after" promises and no invented statistics or prices.
  • Content is complete on the pin itself; we do not publish teaser posts that withhold the answer.
  • Any affiliate relationship is disclosed clearly wherever it applies.
